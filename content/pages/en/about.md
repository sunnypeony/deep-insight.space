## Why this space exists

Most of what I learn about robots comes down to one question: how does a machine turn raw sensor data into a world it can act in? Deep Insight is where I slow down and rebuild each step — from vectors and rotations, to coordinate frames and calibration, to maps and world models — until it makes intuitive sense.

## What I'm working on

- Perception and mapping for a drone platform (Drone Ranger)
- RGB-D and LiDAR data collection and calibration
