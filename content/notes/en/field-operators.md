## The question

How can one symbol, $\nabla$, produce three completely different operations?

## Intuition

- **Gradient**: standing on a hillside, which direction is steepest?
- **Divergence**: in a tiny box, how much more flows out than flows in?
- **Curl**: drop a tiny paddle wheel into the flow — does it spin?

## Formalism

$$
\nabla f,\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf{F},\qquad \nabla\times\mathbf{F}
$$
